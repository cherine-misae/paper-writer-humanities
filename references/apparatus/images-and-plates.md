# Apparatus — Images and Plates

Read whenever an image, facsimile, or artwork reproduction appears.

- Record for each image: source, creator, date, holding institution or publication, identifier, rights status, and any modification (crop, contrast, annotation).
- Do not reproduce an image whose rights status is unknown; describe it and cite the holding record instead.
- Plate numbering is continuous across the manuscript and referenced in prose. Captions carry the metadata above and separate description from interpretation.
- Store files under `figures/` with relative paths; verify legibility in both DOCX and PDF renderings.
