# Quality Gate

Do not declare completion until all blocking gates pass. Counts are descriptive, not substitutes for scholarly judgment.

## G1. Scope and argument

The manifest states the approved question, corpus, scope, approach, language, and access limits. The thesis answers the question and is no stronger than the evidence. Analytical sections make distinct contributions. A serious counterargument, alternative reading, or limitation is addressed.

## G2. Source integrity

Every bibliography item has a verified record and is used. Source classes are distinguishable. Edition, translation, and archive details appear when relevant. Metadata-only access is never represented as full-text reading.

## G3. Quotation integrity

Every quotation was inspected and has a stable locator. Language, translation, omissions, and emphasis are identified where relevant. Snippets, remembered wording, and inaccessible passages do not appear as quotations. Spot-check quotations before delivery.

## G4. Claim traceability

Every consequential factual, interpretive, historical, and attribution claim maps to `evidence_ledger.md`. The cited material supports the level asserted. Primary-text description, scholarly interpretation, and the report's analysis remain distinct. Disagreement and uncertainty remain visible.

## G5. No invented research

There is no simulated evidence, invented passage, fabricated archival discovery, fictional scholarly position, or claim that the AI performed work it did not perform. Incomplete access is disclosed in the relevant section and completion report.

## G6. Citation and document integrity

`report.md` is the canonical manuscript. DOCX and PDF generation succeeds without silently ignoring errors. There are no unresolved citations or cross-references. Citation style is consistent. Rendered DOCX pages and PDF display all scripts, quotations, notes, images, and bibliography entries correctly. If optional LaTeX was requested, it also compiles cleanly.

## G7. Visual integrity, when applicable

Every visual is necessary, sourced, rights-aware, legible, and referenced. Interpretive relations are not presented as documented facts. No decorative or fabricated quantitative visualization appears.

## G8. Human review and status

Front matter identifies Carrel's AI assistance according to policy, does not identify Carrel as the author, recommends qualified human review, and the completion report lists unresolved access, disputed metadata, and claims needing verification.

## Completion report

Report document pages and sections; delivered formats; source counts by class; direct quotations and verified quotations; unresolved access or edition issues; unresolved citations or references; visual inventory; cross-format equivalence; human-review status; quality-gate result; and retained limitations.
