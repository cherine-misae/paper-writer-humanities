# Core 05 — Quality Gate and Completion Report

Do not declare completion until every blocking gate passes. Counts are descriptive, not substitutes for judgment. After G1–G8, run the profile's additional gates (§9).

## G1. Scope and argument
Manifest states the approved question, evidence, scope, profile and variant, language, and access limits. The thesis or research question is no stronger than the evidence. Sections make distinct contributions. A serious counterargument, alternative reading, or limitation is addressed.

## G2. Source integrity
Every bibliography item has a verified record and is used. Classes are distinguishable. Edition, version, translation, or archive details appear when relevant. Metadata-only or abstract-only access is never represented as full reading.

## G3. Quotation integrity
Every quotation was inspected and has a stable locator (as defined by the profile). Omissions, emphasis, translation, or editing are marked. No snippet, remembered wording, or inaccessible passage appears as a quotation. Spot-check before delivery.

## G4. Claim traceability
Every consequential claim maps to `evidence_ledger.md` and the cited material supports the level asserted. The three voices remain distinct. Disagreement and uncertainty stay visible.

## G5. No invented research
No simulated evidence, invented passage, participant, dataset, number, archival discovery, or scholarly position, and no claim that Carrel performed work it did not perform. Incomplete access is disclosed in the relevant section and in the completion report.

## G6. Citation and document integrity
`report.md` is canonical. DOCX and PDF generation succeeded without silently ignored errors. No unresolved citations or cross-references. Citation style is consistent. Rendered pages display all scripts, quotations, notes, tables, images, and bibliography entries. Optional LaTeX compiles cleanly.

## G7. Visual integrity (when applicable)
Every visual is necessary, sourced, rights-aware, legible, referenced, and ledger-linked. Interpretive relations are labeled as interpretation. No decorative or fabricated visualization.

## G8. Human review and status
Front matter discloses Carrel's assistance, does not name Carrel as author, and recommends qualified human review. The completion report lists unresolved access, disputed metadata, and claims needing verification.

## Completion report

Report: profile, variant, secondary profile, genre, citation style, apparatus used; pages and sections; delivered formats; source counts by class; quotations and verified quotations; unresolved access, edition, or data issues; unresolved citations; visual inventory; cross-format equivalence; gates passed (core and profile); human-review status; retained limitations.
